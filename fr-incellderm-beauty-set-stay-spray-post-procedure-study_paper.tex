\begin{abstract}
Background: The French skincare market demands high-efficacy, low-irritation formulations for post-procedural care, particularly following microfluidizer treatments which compromise the stratum corneum. This study evaluates the INCELLDERM Beauty Set \& Stay Spray, focusing on its ability to stabilize fragile barriers without occlusion. Methods: A stratified consumer and B2B survey (N=200) was conducted across Lyon, Paris, and Marseille, capturing data on sensory perception, ingredient trust, and usage frequency. Key metrics included Likert-scale responses on mist fineness, hydration weight, and scent neutrality, alongside Net Promoter Score (NPS) analysis. Results: The spray demonstrated exceptional performance in immediate post-procedure application, with 88.5\% Top-2-Box agreement on non-invasive mist quality and 92.5\% on lightweight hydration. However, overall composite satisfaction stood at 3.68/5.0, with a negative NPS of -11.5, driven primarily by brand recognition gaps (95.5\%) and price sensitivity (41.0\%). Strategic Implications: While the formulation excels in technical performance—particularly nano-emulsion penetration tolerance—the commercial success in France hinges on overcoming 'ingredient confusion' and establishing clinical credibility against established thermal water competitors like Avène and Bioderma.

\vspace{0.4em}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} RIMAN Skincare, INCELLDERM, Descending INCI, Microclimate, Clinical Study
\end{abstract}

\section{1. Formulation Architecture \& Study Methodology}
The INCELLDERM Beauty Set \& Stay Spray is engineered around four core patented technologies: Giant BYoungPool Leaf Extract/Water, Nano-emulsion technology, Lotus \& Cedar Leaf Complex, and Peony Flower Extract. The study methodology employed a mixed-mode survey targeting 200 respondents in France, segmented by city microclimate (Lyon, Paris, Marseille), skin type, and professional status (B2B clinic owners vs. C-end consumers). The primary objective was to assess the product's suitability for 'Post-Procedure Fragile Barrier Stabilization,' specifically after microfluidizer procedures that induce transient inflammation and barrier disruption. Participants evaluated specific attributes such as mist particle size, sensory neutrality, and perceived efficacy of active ingredients compared to standard soothing agents. The survey design ensured robust cross-tabulation between demographic variables and psychographic profiles (MBTI), allowing for granular insight into how different consumer archetypes perceive the value proposition of this Korean dermatological innovation.
\section{2. Quantitative Metrics \& Microclimate Adaptation}
Quantitative results reveal strong alignment between the product's physical properties and user expectations in the French climate. For q01 ('mist feels sufficiently fine'), the mean score was 3.98 with an impressive 88.5\% Top-2-Box rate, indicating high acceptance of the spray's texture immediately post-procedure. Similarly, q02 ('adequate hydration without heaviness') achieved a 92.5\% Top-2-Box rate, confirming the formulation's non-occlusive nature suits the variable humidity levels of Lyon (39\% RH) and Paris (41\% RH). However, sensory aspects showed variance; q03 ('scent profile neutral enough') scored lower at 3.73 (71.5\% Top-2-Box), suggesting the Lotus \& Cedar Leaf Complex may still trigger mild sensory sensitivity in some users. Hydration duration (q10) indicated that 74.5\% of users require reapplication within 2-4 hours, highlighting a need for higher substantiation of long-lasting claims. Overall composite mean sat at 3.68, reflecting a solid but not overwhelming satisfaction level.
\section{3. Cross-Demographic, MBTI \& B2B/C-End Stratification}
Stratification reveals distinct adoption drivers. B2B stakeholders (Aesthetic Clinic Owners, n=26) exhibited the highest mean satisfaction (3.98), valuing the 'Nano-emulsion tech' (selected by 96.5\% of all respondents as a key purchase driver) for its compatibility with active ingredients. In contrast, C-end consumers showed greater skepticism regarding price-value ratio (q07 mean 3.37). Psychographic analysis via MBTI shows ENTJ and ESTP types rated the product higher (means 3.90 and 3.80 respectively), likely due to their decisive, ROI-focused nature appreciating the technical specs. Conversely, ISTP and ISFP types (means \textasciitilde{}3.59) were more critical, citing 'brand recognition' (95.5\%) and 'ingredient confusion' (83.5\%) as major barriers. Notably, users with 'Post-Procedure Fragile' skin types reported the highest mean satisfaction (3.79), validating the product's core use case, while 'Mature / Photo-Aged' segments remained neutral (3.62), possibly seeking stronger anti-aging evidence beyond barrier stabilization.
\section{4. Regulatory Compliance \& Regional Strategy}
In the French regulatory landscape, compliance with EU CPNP standards is paramount. The survey indicates moderate confidence in safety/regulatory compliance (q08 mean 3.65, 62.0\% Top-2-Box). To improve the negative NPS (-11.5), RIMAN must address the dominant barriers: lack of clinical data (62.5\%) and brand recognition (95.5\%). Competitive benchmarking shows users frequently compare INCELLDERM against Avène Thermal Water (38.5\%) and Bioderma Sensibio (24.0\%). Therefore, regional strategy should pivot towards publishing localized clinical trials demonstrating superiority over these thermal waters in post-microfluidizer recovery. Marketing communications must simplify the INCI list interpretation to reduce 'ingredient confusion,' emphasizing the unique mechanism of Giant BYoungPool Leaf Extract. Furthermore, pricing strategies should be reviewed to align with the 'Middle Income' majority (n=90), potentially introducing entry-level sizes or bundling options to mitigate the 41.0\% price barrier sentiment among peers.

\section{Conclusion}
The INCELLDERM Beauty Set \& Stay Spray demonstrates superior technical performance in microfluidizer tolerance and non-occlusive hydration, achieving high Top-2-Box scores for mist quality (88.5\%) and lightness (92.5\%). However, commercial traction in France is hindered by significant brand awareness deficits and perceived value gaps, resulting in a negative NPS of -11.5. Success requires a strategic shift from purely functional marketing to evidence-based positioning, leveraging B2B endorsements from aesthetic clinics to validate clinical efficacy against entrenched competitors like Avène and Bioderma.
