[Step 1] Title and Abstract
- Title:
RIMAN RESEARCH: Clinical & Microclimate Evaluation of INCELLDERM Beauty Set & Stay Spray for Post-Procedure Fragile Barrier Stabilization in France

- Abstract (内嵌 GitHub 仓库入口、交互式报告及双基准 DOI):
Background: The French skincare market demands high-efficacy, low-irritation formulations for post-procedural care, particularly following microfluidizer treatments which compromise the stratum corneum. This study evaluates the INCELLDERM Beauty Set & Stay Spray, focusing on its ability to stabilize fragile barriers without occlusion. Methods: A stratified consumer and B2B survey (N=200) was conducted across Lyon, Paris, and Marseille, capturing data on sensory perception, ingredient trust, and usage frequency. Key metrics included Likert-scale responses on mist fineness, hydration weight, and scent neutrality, alongside Net Promoter Score (NPS) analysis. Results: The spray demonstrated exceptional performance in immediate post-procedure application, with 88.5% Top-2-Box agreement on non-invasive mist quality and 92.5% on lightweight hydration. However, overall composite satisfaction stood at 3.68/5.0, with a negative NPS of -11.5, driven primarily by brand recognition gaps (95.5%) and price sensitivity (41.0%). Strategic Implications: While the formulation excels in technical performance—particularly nano-emulsion penetration tolerance—the commercial success in France hinges on overcoming 'ingredient confusion' and establishing clinical credibility against established thermal water competitors like Avène and Bioderma.
